# Project Statement

## Student College Budget Tracker

### Problem Statement

College students receive money from different sources such as pocket money, scholarships and part-time work. They also spend money on food, books, travel, mobile services, entertainment and other requirements.

When these expenses are not properly tracked, it becomes difficult for a student to understand total spending and whether the available money is sufficient.

The Student College Budget Tracker is developed to provide a simple Python-based solution for this problem.

---

## Proposed Solution

The project takes information about the student's income and expenses as input.

The income is collected from:

- Pocket Money
- Scholarship
- Job Money

The expenses are collected from:

- Food
- Books
- Travel
- Mobile
- Entertainment
- Other Expenses

The program then calculates:

```text
Total Income
Total Expense
Balance
